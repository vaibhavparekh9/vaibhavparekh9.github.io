Where I've been

Built an LLM parser for BTLx timber-fabrication schemas to drive robotic panel-cutting, and stood up an articulated mobile-manipulator simulation in Isaac Sim with MoveIt and Nav2 plus vision-based scene understanding.
Authored URDF/SDF and a ROS2-integrated USD simulation for autonomous docking of a Clearpath Jackal with an inductive charging station, and trained a YOLOv11 detector on synthetic Isaac Sim data using domain randomization for sim-to-real transfer.
My Master's thesis: unsupervised keypoint discovery on multi-viewpoint objects. Also rigged robot models in Blender for visual-SLAM testing in Isaac Sim.
Energy and financial modeling across wind, solar, and storage, and led a project to fine-tune a GPT-3.5 model that summarized and compared long tender documents — cutting analysis time from about 45 minutes to under 10.
Led drivetrain and carbon-fiber bodywork for Yugant, a student-built fuel-efficient prototype for the Shell Eco-marathon — my first taste of taking a machine from CAD to the track.
